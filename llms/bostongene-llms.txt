# BostonGene

> BostonGene is a Waltham, Massachusetts precision-oncology company that pairs an in-house CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited clinical laboratory with an AI-powered bioinformatics platform. It integrates whole-exome and whole-transcriptome sequencing, liquid biopsy (cfDNA/cfRNA), single-cell RNA sequencing, flow cytometry, spatial proteomics and digital pathology into multimodal molecular and immune profiling for cancer patients, providers and biopharma partners.

BostonGene publishes **no public developer API, SDK, OpenAPI specification, MCP server or agent card**. Clinician ordering and report delivery run through an authenticated customer portal (Salesforce Experience Cloud) that requires an account requested through the site. Agents and integrators should treat BostonGene as a documentation-and-contact surface, not a callable API.

## Products (clinical)

- [Tumor Portrait test](https://bostongene.com/providers/tumor-portrait): Comprehensive genomic profiling combined with tumor microenvironment analysis for advanced solid tumors.
- [Immunoprofiling](https://bostongene.com/providers/immunoprofiling): Immune system profiling that stratifies patients by likely treatment response from a blood sample.
- [Immunohistochemistry (IHC) test](https://bostongene.com/providers/immunohistochemistry-ihc-test): IHC biomarker testing.
- [Unknown Primary test](https://bostongene.com/providers/unknown-primary-test): Identifies a tumor's origin using DNA and RNA sequencing.
- [Liquid biopsy](https://bostongene.com/providers/bostongene-liquid-biopsy): cfDNA/cfRNA sequencing.
